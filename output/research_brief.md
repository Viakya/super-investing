## Snapshot
- Business: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Latest results: Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026)
  - Revenue from operations: ₹1,248 crore; YoY +18%. [S3]
  - EBITDA: ₹140 crore with 11.2% margin. [S3]
  - Profit after tax: ₹82 crore. [S3]
  - Order book: ₹3,900 crore. [S3]
  - Exports: 21% of revenue. [S3]
  - Bharuch plant (Gujarat) on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27. [S3]
  - Alternate media report cites revenue ₹1,428 crore. [S1]
- Disclosure limitations: NSE disclosure notes a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (appeal planned) and management commentary on impact; promoter holding 58.2% with 4.1% of promoter holding pledged. Open questions remain on the official filing and reconciliation of revenue figures. [S7] [S8] [S3] [S1]

## Bull case
- Revenue momentum supports FY27 growth path: MD reiterated revenue growth guidance of 15–17% for FY27; Bharuch expansion adds capacity; data-centre cables ~9% of domestic revenue and could double in two years. [S2]
- Copper-price pass-through provides partial margin hedge: About two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, reset with a lag of one to two quarters; company notes no irrational pricing from competitors. [S2]
- Diversified revenue mix adds resilience: Exports accounted for 21% of revenue, providing geographic and mix diversification beyond a domestic cables-only exposure. [S3]

## Bear case
- Copper-price volatility remains a margin risk: Even with pass-through, sustained copper hikes could erode near-term margins. [S2]
- Receivables risk: Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, indicating working-capital stress from large state utilities and potential cash-flow impact if slow payments persist. [S2]
- Tax/liability risk: GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore introduces disputed exposure and near-term cash-out risk despite appeal. [S7]
- Governance/pledge risk: Promoters hold 58.2% with 4.1% of promoter holding pledged, highlighting concentrated ownership and pledge risk that could constrain liquidity or amplify downside in stress scenarios. [S8]

## Open questions
